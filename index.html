Groww Gifting

Problem

Young investors want to gift stocks and investments to friends and family, but current platforms make gifting complicated and unintuitive.

Idea

Create a simple gifting flow inside the Groww ecosystem that lets users send stocks as gifts with a personalized message and seamless onboarding.

Solution

Designed a mobile-first gifting experience where users select a stock, add a message, and send the investment as a digital gift. The receiver can claim it easily by creating a Groww account.

TripSync

Problem

Group travel planning is chaotic. Friends coordinate across multiple apps like WhatsApp, Google Docs, and payment apps which creates friction.

Idea

Build a unified platform where groups can plan trips together, track expenses, vote on plans, and manage itineraries in one place.

Solution

Designed TripSync as a collaborative trip planner that includes group decision tools, itinerary planning, expense splitting, and shared travel updates.
