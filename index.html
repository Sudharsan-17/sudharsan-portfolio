Groww Gifting

Fintech · Consumer
Problem
A gap in corporate gifting (farewells, promotions, weddings) — colleagues awkwardly pool money via UPI chat groups with zero long-term value or emotional impact.
Idea
An investment gifting feature inside Groww — sender picks a recipient, amount, and occasion; funds land in the receiver's Groww Balance; receiver invests in Stocks/MF or withdraws.
