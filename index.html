Groww Digital Gift Card
Problem
Gifting money digitally often feels impersonal and lacks meaning. Users also struggle to introduce friends or family to investing in a simple way.
Idea
Enable users to gift investments through Groww using a digital gift card that can be redeemed directly into an investment account.
Solution
A seamless gifting flow where users choose an amount, personalize the gift, and send an investment gift card that encourages recipients to start investing.

TripSync
Problem
Planning group trips becomes chaotic due to scattered conversations, conflicting preferences, and difficulty finalizing decisions.
Idea
Create a collaborative platform where friends can vote on destinations, coordinate plans, and align on travel decisions in one place.
Solution
TripSync simplifies group travel by centralizing trip discussions, enabling quick polls for decisions, and organizing plans into one shared workspace.
